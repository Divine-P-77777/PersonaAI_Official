# AskMentor - Promotional Video Script

## 🎯 Video Overview
- **Target Audience:** Mentors, Industry Experts, and Students
- **Primary Tool:** InVideo AI
- **Format:** Fast-paced, professional, and modern (YouTube/LinkedIn format)
- **Visual Style:** High-quality AI stock footage mixed with your own crisp OBS screen recordings.

---

## 🤖 InVideo AI Prompt Suggestion
*Copy and paste this into InVideo AI to generate the base video:*
> "Create a 60-second professional promotional video for a platform called 'AskMentor.online'. The platform allows top industry experts to create an AI clone of themselves to mentor students 24/7. Use modern, dynamic background music. The tone should be inspiring, tech-forward, and professional. Use high-quality stock footage of frustrated students studying late, busy professionals working on laptops, and futuristic AI visuals. Leave space in the middle for me to overlay my own screen recordings."

---

## 📝 Script & Visual flow

### Scene 1: The Hook (0:00 - 0:10)
**Visual (InVideo AI):** 
Fast cuts. A stressed student looking at a laptop late at night. Then, a busy professional checking their watch or overwhelmed with emails.
**Voiceover:** 
"Students need guidance 24/7. But top mentors? They’re busy building their own careers. What if you could clone your expertise and mentor thousands of students... while you sleep?"

### Scene 2: The Introduction (0:10 - 0:20)
**Visual (InVideo AI):** 
Sleek, glowing AI networks, futuristic data flowing into a human silhouette. Text on screen: "Meet AskMentor.online".
**Voiceover:** 
"Welcome to AskMentor. The revolutionary platform where industry experts train a lifelike AI version of themselves to guide, teach, and review."

### Scene 3: The Power of AskMentor (0:20 - 0:40)
*(This is where you overlay your high-quality OBS screen recordings)*

**OBS Clip 1: The Explore Page & Unlock Flow**
*Show smooth scrolling through the beautiful mentor cards and clicking 'Unlock'.*
**Voiceover:** "Students can browse top mentors and unlock personalized chat and voice sessions instantly."

**OBS Clip 2: The RAG Chat Interface**
*Show the chat interface where the AI gives a highly technical, context-aware answer.*
**Voiceover:** "Powered by advanced RAG technology, your AI clone knows your exact background, past experiences, and coding knowledge."

**OBS Clip 3: Resume Review & Voice Call**
*Show dragging and dropping a PDF resume, and the AI giving feedback. Briefly show the voice call UI.*
**Voiceover:** "It doesn't just chat. It analyzes PDF resumes in seconds and even conducts live voice calls, just like a real human."

### Scene 4: The Creator Economy (0:40 - 0:50)
**Visual (InVideo AI):** 
A confident professional smiling, looking at a rising graph or a wallet notification.
**Voiceover:** 
"Best of all? Mentors get paid. Set your own pricing tiers, offer free monthly explorations, and earn passive income while scaling your impact."

### Scene 5: Call to Action (0:50 - 1:00)
**Visual (InVideo AI & Custom Logo):** 
AskMentor logo glowing in the center. Website URL pulsing at the bottom.
**Voiceover:** 
"Stop trading time for impact. Clone your knowledge today. Visit AskMentor.online and create your AI persona now."
